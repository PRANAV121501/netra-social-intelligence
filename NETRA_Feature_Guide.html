What is NETRA?

NETRA is an AI-powered Social Intelligence Platform that watches social media in real time and converts raw data into clear, actionable intelligence. Think of it like a smart command center that reads millions of posts, finds patterns humans would miss, and gives analysts precise answers — not just charts.


Most social media tools just show you what happened. NETRA tells you why it happened, who caused it, and what's coming next.

Here are the key questions NETRA can answer instantly:
📡
What topics are trending right now?
NETRA tracks topic velocity — how fast a subject is growing — and shows you which ones are rising, peaking, or fading in real time.
🧬
What narratives are emerging?
It detects the stories being told across posts — not just keywords — and groups them into named narratives with a clear stance (positive, negative, neutral).
👑
Who are the most influential users?
Using a graph algorithm (similar to Google's PageRank), NETRA ranks which accounts have the most real-world impact on the conversation.
🌐
How is information spreading?
NETRA traces the exact path a piece of content travels — from the original poster to every person who amplified it — and measures its spread speed.
🤖
Are there bots or fake accounts?
An automated detection engine gives every account a "bot probability score" and identifies coordinated groups of fake accounts working together.
🔮
What topics will trend next?
NETRA's predictive engine forecasts topic trajectories 24 to 72 hours into the future — so analysts can act before a crisis, not after.

The 10-Stage Intelligence Pipeline
Every time NETRA analyzes data, it passes through 10 specialist AI engines — one after another — each adding a new layer of intelligence. Here's what each engine does in plain language:
01
Entity Extractor
Reads every post and picks out the important "things" — people's names, organizations, locations, and topic tags like IndiaAI, CyberSecurity, StockRally, NationalSecurity, and ClimateAction. Like a highlighter that marks the most important words automatically.
02
Sentiment Engine
Goes beyond "positive / negative" and measures 5 emotions in each post: Fear, Anger, Joy, Trust, and Urgency. A post about a cyberattack might score high on Fear and Urgency — this nuance matters for real intelligence work.
03
Topic Engine
Tracks every topic's velocity — how fast it's growing — and assigns a lifecycle stage: Emerging → Accelerating → Peaking → Stabilizing → Decaying. Knowing a topic is "Accelerating" tells analysts to pay close attention right now.
04
Narrative Engine
A narrative is a story being told, not just a keyword. This engine groups posts into named narratives (e.g., "AI Regulation Debate", "Data Privacy Concerns") and identifies who is pushing each story and what angle they're taking.
05
Community Detection Engine
Finds natural clusters of accounts that interact with each other — using the Louvain algorithm (a graph math technique). Each cluster gets a label like "Tech Journalists", "Policy Advocates", or "Amplifier Network". Understanding communities reveals who is coordinating.
06
Influence Engine
Calculates who really matters in the conversation — not just by follower count, but by how central they are in the network. Uses a custom PageRank algorithm (same math Google uses to rank web pages) plus betweenness centrality to find "bridge" accounts connecting different communities.
07
Propagation Engine
Traces how content spreads from its origin. Builds cascade trees showing the exact path of retweets/shares, measures diffusion speed, and calculates an R₀ virality index (borrowed from epidemiology) — R₀ above 1.0 means the content is "going viral".
08
Graph Engine
Builds the full Knowledge Graph — a visual map of the entire social landscape. Nodes represent accounts and topics; edges represent interactions. This is what powers the constellation network visualization you see on the dashboard.
09
Bot Detection Engine
Gives every account a bot probability score (0–100). Accounts score higher if they post at inhuman speeds, have suspicious follower/following ratios, or show copy-paste behavior. Also detects Coordinated Inauthentic Behavior (CIB) — groups of fake accounts working as a team.
10
Predictive Engine
The final stage looks at all the data and forecasts the future — which topics will trend in the next 24, 48, and 72 hours, with a confidence percentage. This gives decision-makers time to respond before situations escalate.

AI Assistant — Ask Questions in Plain English
The AI Assistant is one of NETRA's most powerful features. Instead of navigating menus or running queries, analysts simply type a question — and NETRA understands, routes it to the right engine, and returns a structured answer in seconds.
No technical knowledge needed. The assistant understands natural language — you don't need to know how to code, write SQL, or understand graph theory to get deep intelligence out of NETRA.
Example questions you can ask:
Who are the most influential accounts in the CyberSecurity cluster?
What caused the IndiaAI narrative to spike today?
Are there any coordinated bot campaigns active right now?
How did this content spread — show me the cascade path?
Which topics are likely to trend in the next 48 hours?
Which community is driving the StockRally conversation?
The assistant handles 6 query categories: Influence Analysis, Community Insights, Trend Root Cause, Cascade/Propagation, Bot Alerts, and Predictive Forecasting. Quick-action chips at the top let users one-click common queries without typing.
6
Bot Detection & Threat Intelligence
NETRA's bot detection is built for real-world government and law enforcement use — not just catching obvious spam bots, but detecting sophisticated coordinated influence operations.
🔍
Individual Account Scoring
Every account gets a bot probability score from 0 to 100. Red flags include: posting at inhuman frequency, suspicious follower-to-following ratios, repetitive copy-paste content, and account age vs. activity patterns.
🕸️
CIB Cluster Detection
Coordinated Inauthentic Behavior (CIB) — groups of accounts that work together to artificially amplify content. NETRA finds these clusters by analyzing synchronized posting times, shared hashtag usage, and mutual amplification patterns.
⚠️ Why This Matters for SIH Judges
This is directly relevant to India's national security challenges. Foreign influence operations, election interference campaigns, and astroturfing attacks use exactly these techniques. NETRA can detect them automatically — something no basic social media dashboard can do.
